📘 How to Play PEAK HYPE

Overview & Goal

Two rival companies compete to turn a new service/product into a cultural phenomenon across 2 Chapters (30 total cards).

Turn Actions

Draft any uncovered card from the pyramid and choose to either:

  • Play Card: Pay its Budget cost (coins) to trigger its market expansion, virality, or tag effect.
  • Discard Card: Discard it to immediately gain +2 Budget.

Market Districts & Locking

Placing a token adds brand presence. Placing a 2nd token in the same district LOCKS it (creates a 2-token shield immune to removal). Placing 1 token in a district with 1 rival token removes their token instead.

Subculture Tag Pair Bonus

Collecting a 2nd copy of the same Subculture tag (TECH, CULTURE, ECO, LIFESTYLE) grants an immediate +2 Budget bonus!

3 Instant Win Conditions

  1. Total Hype: Pull the Hype Marker onto your end space (-3 for A, +3 for B).
  2. Cultural Monopoly: Collect all 4 unique Subculture Tags.
  3. Market Control: Control at least 1 token in all 4 Districts simultaneously (or majority control at end of Chapter II).
